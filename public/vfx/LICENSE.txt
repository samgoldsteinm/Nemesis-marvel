flash_atlas.png is built from textures in Binbun3D's "Muzzle Flash VFX" pack (flash_front_01, flash_front_03,
flash_side_01, flash_long_01), downscaled and packed into a 2x2 grayscale atlas.

The effects in src/vfx.ts are GLSL re-implementations inspired by Binbun3D's free Godot packs:
Stylized Hit FX, Electric FX, Stylized Explosion FX, Elemental Magic FX, Status FX, Magic Projectiles VFX,
Beam VFX and Muzzle Flash VFX.
